## 2. Information We Process

### Information stored on your device

- Onboarding profile information, such as the name or nickname you enter, trading experience, market focus, trading style, and selected challenge.
- App preferences, including language, selected agents, agent customizations, onboarding status, and subscription-preview settings.
- Up to 30 recent analysis records and associated chart images.
- Up to 40 saved follow-up turns for each analysis.

This locally stored information remains in the app’s private storage until you delete it through available controls, it is removed by the app’s automatic limits, or you uninstall the app.

### Information submitted for analysis

- A chart image that you deliberately select using Apple’s system photo picker.
- Analysis settings, including selected agents, language, whether news context is requested, detected or selected symbol and timeframe, and agent-profile settings.
- Follow-up questions, prior follow-up context, and the generated analysis needed to answer the question.
- AI-generated analysis results and responses.

### Subscription information

Our subscription-management provider may process an anonymous app user identifier, Apple receipt or transaction token, product and entitlement identifiers, purchase and renewal history, subscription status, country or storefront information, and technical device information needed to validate and manage purchases. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or bank-account details.

### Technical and support information

- Internet Protocol address, request date and time, request path, response status, user-agent or device/network information, and security or error logs generated when you communicate with our servers.
- Your email address, message, attachments, and related correspondence if you contact customer support or submit a privacy request.

## 5. Storage and Retention

- Chart images on our application server: stored only in a temporary working directory during the analysis request and deleted when processing finishes. We do not maintain a server-side analysis-history database containing the original image.
- Analysis history on your device: retained until you delete an individual record, use the in-app delete-all control, the app removes older records beyond its limits, or you uninstall the app.
- On-device profile and preferences: retained until changed or until the app is uninstalled.
- Server access, security, and error logs: retained for up to one year.
- Server backups: retained for up to one year. Temporary chart-image files are not intentionally included as standalone retained records, but limited residual copies may persist temporarily in infrastructure backups or provider systems where technically unavoidable.
- AI-processing data: retained under the AI provider’s applicable service and safety rules. Model-improvement sharing has been disabled by us where available. Under default API abuse-monitoring controls, relevant content may be retained for up to 30 days unless longer retention is required by law or applies to a specific service feature. Zero Data Retention is not enabled.
- Subscription and transaction information: retained for as long as needed to administer subscriptions, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and satisfy Apple, provider, accounting, tax, and legal requirements.
- Support correspondence: retained until the request is resolved and thereafter for up to three years where reasonably necessary for dispute handling or legal compliance.

When a retention period expires, information under our control is deleted or de-identified using reasonable technical methods, unless continued retention is required by law.
